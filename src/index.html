About Me

Hi, I'm Ichimaki Kasura.


I enjoy building things, solving technical problems, and experimenting with new ideas. My interests include embedded systems, computer hardware, programming, and the occasional side project. I also enjoy playing guitar, traveling, and exploring new places.


I'm the founder of IchiProtoTech, an online platform and freelance service that develops embedded systems and functional prototypes for students and hobbyists using Arduino, ESP32, and Raspberry Pi. I also provide basic computer repair and technical support.


I've also conducted an Arduino workshop titled "THINK. WIRE. CODE. The Arduino Starter Training", where I introduced students to the fundamentals of Arduino programming, electronics, and hands-on prototyping.


While I don't code as much as I used to, I still enjoy developing software, custom tools, and embedded projects—using web development mainly to showcase what I build.
